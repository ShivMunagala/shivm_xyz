Works In Progress
Independent research projects · 2026
  • ARC White-Box Estimation Challenge: Competing in the Alignment Research Center (ARC)'s contest on compute-efficient methods for estimating a neural network's behaviour directly from its weights. Placed 3rd of 78 in the warm-up round and provisionally 27th of 312 in Phase 1; Phase 2 ongoing.
  • Failure modes of a Probe-Then-Ablate pipeline (adapted from a Bluedot TAIS Puzzle): Found that ablating a linear probe's direction can look causally inert on an undertrained model but causally relevant once it has converged, with the change happening in line with training loss and driven by a redundancy gap between how many directions decode a feature and how many the network actually uses. SAE-based probes resist the same construction. Post being written up.
  • Geometric Test for Multi-Dimensional Structure in SAE Latents: Exploring a rotation-based permutation-null diagnostic for detecting multi-dimensional feature geometry in sparse autoencoder latents, run on Pythia-70M. Experiment underway.
  • Failing to Improve on the Muon Optimizer: Looked for cheaper alternatives to Muon's orthogonalisation step (QDWH, Cholesky QR, low-rank sketches). Found the momentum matrices are ill-conditioned so their effective rank collapses during training, explaining why the existing coarse-but-robust design is well matched to the problem. Backburner, undecided whether to write this up.
